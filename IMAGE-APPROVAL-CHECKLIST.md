## Review each hairstyle

- [ ] Cut, length, texture, layering, curl pattern, volume, and color match the original closely.
- [ ] Rear view only; no recognizable face in the image or a reflection.
- [ ] Hair strands, scalp, neck, shoulders, clothing, and lighting appear realistic.
- [ ] No unrelated people, clutter, labels, text, or watermark.
- [ ] Background and warm-neutral lighting are consistent across the set.
- [ ] Crop is 4:5, subject scale is consistent, and file is 1200 × 1500 WebP.
- [ ] Edited candidate does not materially alter the actual hairstyle.
- [ ] Generated candidate is labeled representative and is not attributed to an actual customer.

## Dominique portrait candidate

- [ ] Facial structure, skin tone, eyes, nose, lips, smile, and recognizable likeness are preserved.
- [ ] Hairstyle, denim clothing, and jewelry are preserved.
- [ ] Makeup, lighting, and background look refined but realistic.
- [ ] No excessive smoothing, reshaping, age alteration, text, or watermark.
- [ ] Dominique and Javay approve it before the website portrait source is changed.
